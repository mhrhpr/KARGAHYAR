# KARGAHYAR Brand System

## Identity
Brand: کارگاهیار / KARGAHYAR
Tagline: «کارگاهت را مدیریت کن، نه کاغذها را.»

## Visual language
- Background: #0B0F14
- Surface: #121923
- Border: #26303B
- Accent: #2563EB
- Primary text: #F5F7FA
- Muted text: #AEB6C2

## Mark
The K-shaped construction mark is the canonical symbol. Do not replace it with a generic hammer, house, crane or accounting icon.

## SEO naming
Use «کارگاهیار» as the primary Persian brand name and “KARGAHYAR” as the Latin alternate. Product copy should naturally include high-intent construction/workshop management terms without keyword stuffing.

## Required consistency
The same colors, mark geometry, naming and tagline must be retained across mobile, web, app icon, metadata and future marketing material.
